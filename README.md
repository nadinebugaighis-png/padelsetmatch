# Matchapp

can you built a web app, that combines finding a dating match, using ai to indicate the best match according to age preference, background/ nationality or neighbouring country similarities and culture, also asking what is someone priorities ranked (such as what's more important, intellectual, looks, money, social butterfly, fun, quiet, preserved, fashionable, funny, adventurous) and of course they will have to be Padel players, it doesn't matter their level but they have to love the game and are willing to play a match.  now this plus the smartness of ai using location to identify potential matches using the existing app for example playtomic to arrange for them to meet and play a match together, the idea is that people who play together stay together, its an enjoyable sport, social and gives people a chance to get to know certain traits of the potential partner.  I prefer to start the app in madrid, to see how it works.  and worst case scenario they can become potential friends
