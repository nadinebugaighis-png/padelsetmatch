Subject: Resubmission — Build 14 addresses all three issues

Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for the detailed feedback on Version 1.0 (Build 13). We have fixed all three items and are resubmitting Build 14.

1. Guideline 2.1(a) — Sign in with Apple unresponsive
Root cause found and fixed. The third-party Capacitor plugin we used was built for an older Capacitor version and was silently excluded from the build, so the button had no native implementation behind it. We removed that dependency and implemented Sign in with Apple natively in the app with ASAuthorizationController, added the Sign in with Apple entitlement to the app target, and set an explicit presentation anchor so the sheet appears correctly on iPad. Tested on iPad and iPhone.

2. Guideline 1.2 — User-generated content / EULA
The sign-in and registration screen now shows a required agreement checkbox before an account can be created or a session started: "I agree to the Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy, and I understand there is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users." Both documents are linked directly from that screen. The Terms page now has a dedicated zero-tolerance section stating that objectionable content and abusive behaviour are prohibited, that reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and that offending content is removed and accounts suspended or banned. Every post, comment, message and profile has a flag icon to report content, and any user can be blocked from their profile.

3. Guideline 5.1.1(v) — Account deletion
In-app account deletion is available and has been made more prominent: Me tab -> Settings -> "Delete my account" (full-width red button at the bottom), with a single confirmation step. It permanently deletes the profile, photos, matches, chats and the authentication account. No email, phone call or website visit is required.
