# PadelSetMatch — Safety & Trust

*Maintained by Moorish Arches S.L. Last updated: August 2026.*

PadelSetMatch is a padel players' directory: people find neighbours to play with, join open matches and share free-court access. It is **not** a dating or friendship app — there are no romantic or relationship features anywhere in the product.

---

## 1. Who can see you

- **Sign-in required.** Only verified, signed-in members can browse players. Anonymous sign-ups are disabled and email addresses must be confirmed.
- **No public profiles.** Player cards are never indexed by Google or visible to the open web.
- **Row-level database security.** Every table enforces per-user access rules at the database layer, so a member can only read the data the app is allowed to show them — not by trusting the app, but enforced by the database itself.
- **You control your address.** We ask for a neighbourhood/zone or club, never a home address. Exact GPS coordinates are never shown to other players.
- **Away mode & hiding.** You can pause your visibility or hide individual players from your grid at any time.

## 2. Blocking, hiding and reporting

- **Block** any member: you disappear from each other's grid, matches, chats and Connect feed instantly, and previous relationship data between you is cleaned up.
- **Hide** anyone you simply don't want to see, without blocking them.
- **Report** any profile, post, comment, kit item or chat message in one tap, with a reason and category. Reports go straight to the moderation queue.
- Reporting and blocking are always available from the profile and from every piece of content — as required by app-store safety guidelines.

## 3. Moderation

- **Photo checks.** Profile photos carry a moderation status; anything flagged or reported can be removed and the profile marked so it cannot be shown again unchanged.
- **Admin removal tools.** Administrators can review and permanently remove profile photos, kit photos, Connect posts, comments and chat messages, and can suspend or reinstate an account.
- **Proactive review.** Beyond user reports, moderators browse the newest photos, kit uploads, posts and comments so problem content can be pulled before anyone complains.
- **Suspension.** A suspended account instantly disappears from discovery, matches and messaging.
- Objectionable content and the member who posted it can be removed within minutes of a report — 24/7 commitment for store compliance.

## 4. Messaging safety

- **No cold DMs.** You cannot message someone until a connection exists; an intro is limited to a single opening message until the other person accepts.
- **Accept or ignore.** Every intro can be silently ignored — the sender is not told, so there's no pressure and no retaliation.
- **Delete conversations.** You can delete any thread from your side at any time.
- **Moderator visibility on report only.** Chats are private; a message is only surfaced to a moderator when it is reported, and removed messages are replaced with a "removed by moderator" placeholder.

## 5. Real-world play safety

- **Reliability signals.** Members confirm matches they actually played, and can report a no-show. Played counts and no-show counts are visible so you know who turns up.
- **Anonymous, verified coach reviews.** Only players a coach has confirmed coaching can rate them, and the rating stays anonymous — real feedback without fear.
- **Group chat per match.** Every open match has its own chat so plans stay in one place and nobody needs to share a phone number.
- **Private home courts are flagged.** When a host sets the match location as a private home or property, every player sees a clear "Private court" notice on the match before joining — so nobody turns up at a stranger's house without knowing. Residential complex or urbanisation courts open to residents are not counted as private.
- **Guests are limited.** Guest participants join a single match through a one-off link; they cannot browse the member directory.

## 6. Your data

- **Data controller:** Moorish Arches S.L. (Spain), GDPR jurisdiction.
- **We do not read your private messages.** Our policy contains an explicit no-read clause; access happens only when content is reported.
- **Delete everything.** One action in Settings permanently deletes your account and associated data — profile, photos, matches, messages, ratings.
- **Analytics are optional.** Crash and usage reporting can be switched off in Profile → Privacy, and when off, nothing is queued or sent.
- **No selling, no ad tracking.** Your data is never sold and is not used for cross-app advertising tracking.
- **Encrypted in transit.** All traffic runs over HTTPS; passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form.
- **Sign-in options:** email + password (with leak-checked passwords), Google and Apple sign-in.

## 7. Age and conduct

- **18+ only.** The app is for adults; under-18 accounts are not permitted.
- **Zero tolerance** for nudity, sexual content, harassment, insults, hate speech, spam and commercial scams. Breaking these rules gets content removed and the account suspended.
- **No dating.** Romantic advances are treated as harassment and handled like any other violation.

## 8. Getting help

- In-app **Feedback** and **Report** are the fastest routes.
- Support page: https://padelsetmatch.com/support
- Privacy policy and terms are linked in the app footer and in Settings.
